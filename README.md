## Selected projects

**[RouteForge](https://github.com/amansingh498/RouteForge)**: Logistics dispatch platform that plans multi-depot delivery routes under fleet-capacity constraints. Benchmarks Dijkstra, Nearest Neighbour, 2-Opt and Held-Karp on road-distance matrices.
`C++` `Python` `FastAPI` `React` `OSRM`

**[NadiRakshak](https://github.com/amansingh498/NadiRakshak)** ([live demo](https://nadi-rakshak-ruby.vercel.app)): River health monitoring and incident accountability platform for Indian rivers. Scores water quality against CPCB criteria, fills sensor gaps with Sentinel-2 satellite turbidity (NDTI), clusters pollution hotspots with DBSCAN, and tracks citizen complaints through an 8-stage authority lifecycle with SLA alerts and pHash-based duplicate-photo detection.
`Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `React` `TypeScript` `Docker`

**[JobScout](https://github.com/amansingh498/JobScout)** ([live demo](https://job-scout-ashen.vercel.app)): Job and internship research agent that finds listings, researches missing details like stipend and work mode, parses your resume for skill-match scoring, and audits postings for ghost-job risk before ranking them with a transparent 100-point score.
`Python` `FastAPI` `Next.js` `Gemini API`

**[IndiaStockLens](https://github.com/amansingh498/IndiaStockLens)**: Equity research platform that turns one ticker into a live brief by collecting and normalising data from Yahoo Finance, NSE, Screener.in and SEBI, with async fetching, caching and source-health tracking.
`Python` `FastAPI` `Pydantic` `Docker` `React`
